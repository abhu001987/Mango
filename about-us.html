About MapiDesk

See it on the map. Remember it for the exam.

MapiDesk is an interactive map-based quiz platform for geography, history, polity, economics, and current affairs. Instead of reading through pages of notes, you click on a live map, answer short quizzes, and let the places themselves teach you where things are and why they matter.

1,800+ locations 2,100+ questions Built for exam prep

What it does

MapiDesk turns static geography into something you interact with. Every quiz is tied to an actual location on the map — straits, rivers, mountain ranges, borders — so instead of memorizing a name in isolation, you learn where it sits and how it connects to everything around it.

Questions cover geography as well as history, polity, economics, and current affairs, making it useful for general learning and for competitive exam preparation, where map-based questions are common and easy to lose marks on.

How it helps

Learn by doing
Click a place, answer a question, get instant feedback. Active recall sticks far better than reading a map once and moving on.
Built for revision
Short quizzes and XP progress make repeat revision feel manageable, so you can fit it into small pockets of time instead of long study blocks.
Fact-checked content
Every location and question is verified before it goes live, so what you're learning is accurate and exam-ready.
